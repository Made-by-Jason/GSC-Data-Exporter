Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool secure?

Yes. This tool runs entirely in your web browser. Your Google Search Console data is never sent to any server, ensuring your data remains private and secure.

What are the export limits?

The Google Search Console API allows for up to 25,000 rows per request. This tool uses that limit, which is much higher than the 1,000-row limit in the GSC web interface.
